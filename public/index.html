Travel & Stay

Getting to Morro Bay

Morro Bay sits on Highway 1 on California’s Central Coast, about halfway between Los Angeles and San Francisco.

By Air

The closest airport is San Luis Obispo (SBP), about 20 minutes away. Other options are Santa Barbara (SBA) or flying into LA or the Bay Area and making a road trip of it.

By Car

Morro Bay is roughly 3½–4 hours from Los Angeles or the San Francisco Bay Area. The drive up Highway 1 or 101 is half the fun.

Explore

Make a long weekend of it

Memorial Day Monday gives you an extra day. Here are a few of our favorite ways to spend it.

Paddle

Kayak the Bay

Rent a kayak or paddleboard on the Embarcadero and glide past the sand spit, the herons and Morro Rock.

Say hello to

The Sea Otters

Morro Bay’s harbor is home to a raft of sea otters. Look for them floating on their backs near the Embarcadero and the rock.

Hike

Hills & Bluffs

Climb Black Hill for the best view of the bay, or wander the wild bluffs and tide pools of Montaña de Oro State Park.

Wander

Morro Rock & the Embarcadero

Walk the waterfront for chowder, smoked fish and salt-water taffy, then head out to the base of the 576-foot rock.

Day trip

Up & Down the Coast

Hearst Castle, the elephant seals at Piedras Blancas, sleepy Cayucos and Cambria, downtown San Luis Obispo, and the wineries of Paso Robles are all an easy drive.
